# Keagan & Cindy Wedding Invitation

## Event details
- 6 November 2026 — Wedding Ceremony — GBKP Berastagi Kota
- 7 November 2026 — Traditional Party — JAMBUR MILALAMAS

## Upload
Put `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, and the `assets` folder at the ROOT of your GitHub Pages repository.

## Photos
Replace the placeholder SVG files in `assets/` with your photos. If using JPGs, change the corresponding `.svg` filenames in `style.css` to `.jpg`.

## Music
Put your song at `assets/music.mp3`. The guest taps OPEN INVITATION, which starts the music. This interaction is intentional because mobile browsers generally block autoplay with sound.

## RSVP
Create a Google Form and replace `RSVP_URL` at the top of `script.js`.

## Countdown
The countdown currently uses 6 November 2026 at 10:00 AM Jakarta time as a placeholder ceremony time. Change `WEDDING_DATE` when the exact ceremony time is confirmed.


## Wedding Gift
The new `#gift` section is ready. Replace the account name, bank name, account number, and contact email in `index.html`.
For privacy, consider whether you want to publish bank details publicly. A QR code or payment link can also be used.
